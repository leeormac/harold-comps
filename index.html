Recommendations

Deepen the concession on a standard term, hold the base rent, fix the feeds

Pricing

  1. Move the free month to a 12 month term and drop the 10 day move in deadline. Same cost per lease, a deeper monthly number on the listing, and no friction the comps do not impose. Fusion, El Centro and 5420 Sunset all run their concession on a standard term.
  2. Unit 214: hold $3,394 base, one month free on 12. Net $3,111, $2.65/sf. $55 under Fusion's 1,275 sf unit, $316 over El Centro, with parking and 1,176 sf.
  3. Unit 315: reconcile the feeds, then $3,495 base, one month free on 12. Net $3,204. Between Fusion's two plans and under all three Archer units.
  4. PH 411: $3,695 base, one month free on 12. Net $3,387. $288 under Fusion's 991 sf unit and $203 under Archer's 910 sf, with a private deck. This unit has been listed since March; the current $3,850 base is a $155 cut.
  5. Concessions, not base cuts, everywhere else. Under the RSO the move in rent caps every increase for that tenancy. A free month costs one month. A base cut compounds for as long as the tenant stays.
  6. Studios hold at $2,095. They are at market against Fusion's 479 sf studio at $2,249 and are 50 sf larger. Pull the $2,300 placeholder units.
  7. Three bedrooms to $3,795 base. Hollywood 3BR market sits at $3,855 to $3,995 and 1,238 sf is small for the type. Market them to roommates at under $1,300 a bedroom.
  8. Window: 60 days. Ten units into December leasing costs more than one month free on each.

Marketing

  1. One pricing basis on every feed. Zillow shows the 16 month net average, RentCafe shows base with no special, Apartments.com shows a 12 month spread on 411 only. Unit 411 reads $3,437, $3,609 and $3,850 across the three today and unit 315 does not reconcile at all. Pick gross plus a stated special, push it to every feed, and retire the per unit MLS "condo for rent" listings.
  2. Delete the WAIT5 placeholder rows. Twenty nine plan rows on RentCafe make a 56 unit building read as empty and anchor prospects on the highest price.
  3. Lead with size and quiet. 1,176 sf against 907 to 1,081 at the comps. Reviews score noise 4.5 and parking 5.0. Say both in the first line.
  4. Push the Zillow photo set everywhere. Zillow carries 57 photos and a 3D tour. RentCafe carries ten with raw phone file names and no tour, and that is the feed that syndicates to the Yardi network.
  5. Tour hours. Comps show 9 to 6 six or seven days. Harold shows 10 to 4 weekdays and 10 to 2 weekends. Add evening and self guided tours.
  6. Fold water and trash into rent. Comps show a single total monthly price. RUBS on top of a base rent reads as a $100 surprise.
  7. Reframe the condo notice. Script the leasing conversation around RSO protection and a future first right to buy before a prospect reads "condo for rent" and assumes displacement.
  8. Locator and broker fees through December. Half a month on ten units is cheaper than ten more vacant months.
